About

I am a Machine Learning researcher working under the guidance of Dr. Soumitra Samanta. My research interests span Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Cheminformatics, Graph Representation Learning, and Machine Unlearning. I am particularly interested in developing effective learning algorithms and representation methods for structured and multimodal data, with applications in computational chemistry and scientific discovery. My broader interests also include trustworthy and reliable AI, molecular representation learning, and multimodal AI systems.
